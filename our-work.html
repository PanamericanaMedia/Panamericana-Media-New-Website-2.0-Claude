Crafted for Impact

Our portfolio isn't just logos — it's a record of organizations we've helped connect more clearly with the communities they're trying to reach.

From communications consulting to bilingual storytelling, every project below reflects one belief: culturally resonant communication — built from the community up — consistently outperforms direct translation.

Government & Public Sector Strategy + Production In Progress

City of Aurora — Office of International & Immigrant Affairs

Our flagship engagement and the clearest example of how strategy and production work together at Panamericana Media: a consulting relationship that opened the door, followed by a full bilingual production project built on that trust.

Phase 1 — Communications Strategy Consulting

Communication strategy consulting focused on community engagement and outreach. We participated in research discussions and stakeholder meetings around improving communication with immigrant and refugee communities, and provided recommendations on multilingual communications, trusted community partnerships, and community-centered storytelling — supporting the city's engagement efforts alongside Conecta Colorado.

Phase 2 — IRA Canopy Project (Currently in Production)

A community-focused video highlighting Aurora's urban tree canopy initiative, funded through the Inflation Reduction Act. We're working with city staff and community stakeholders to communicate the environmental, public health, and neighborhood benefits of expanding tree canopy in underserved areas — leading story development, interviews, filming, editing, and final production.

The video is being produced in two distinct versions — English and Spanish — each with a different storytelling approach built for how that audience receives the message, not a translated script.

Government & Public Sector Event & Production

Denver Mayor's Office — 2025 Design Awards

We produced the opening video for the 2025 Denver Mayor's Design Awards, collaborating with the City and County of Denver and project partners to showcase the impact of design across Denver communities. We led story development, interviews, filming, editing, and final video production for the official event opener, hosted by Mayor Mike Johnston and Denver Community Planning and Development.

Nonprofit & Community Strategic Storytelling

Juntos Community

We produced Juntos Community's 3rd Anniversary Impact Video, capturing powerful participant stories and celebrating the organization's impact. We also produced the Juntos Run Club impact story, highlighting how the program builds community and belonging.

Beyond production, we supported the organization with strategic storytelling and bilingual content designed to strengthen community engagement, advocacy, and fundraising efforts.

Nonprofit & Community Documentary & Photography

Brothers Redevelopment

We produced a series of documentary-style client stories highlighting housing stability and homelessness prevention, along with fundraising and impact videos centered on real client experiences. Photography and visual storytelling support rounded out the engagement.

Every piece was built to communicate the organization's mission through authentic, human-centered storytelling — not stock imagery standing in for real people.

Nonprofit & Community Strategy + Production

Scholars Unlimited

We provided communication strategy consulting alongside production of the organization's annual gala fundraising video. We also developed a LinkedIn content strategy and messaging recommendations to strengthen the organization's visibility with donors and institutional partners.

The storytelling content highlights student success and organizational impact for the audiences that matter most.

Nonprofit & Community Organizational Storytelling

Latino Leadership Institute

We produced an organizational statement video for the Latino Leadership Institute, launched at one of the organization's gala events — a clear, focused piece of storytelling built to represent the institute's mission and impact to the community gathered to support it.

Business & Brand Work

Real estate, banking, hospitality, media, and food brands working to build genuine relationships with Latino and Hispanic audiences.

Media & Events Multi-Year Partnership

Westword

A multi-year media partnership covering Westword's flagship events — Tacolandia, Out to Brunch, and Westword Feast. We've handled promotional event coverage video and photography, along with bilingual testimonials capturing the energy, culture, and community of each event in both English and Spanish.

Repeat, multi-event trust is one of the clearest signals of what working with us looks like over time — not a one-off shoot, but a partner Westword keeps coming back to year after year.

Finance & Sponsorship Multi-Partner Collaboration

Alpine Bank — Hispanic Heritage Month 2024

Produced in partnership with Conecta Colorado, a Spanish-language media network we work closely with, and sponsored by Alpine Bank for Hispanic Heritage Month 2024. This project illustrates how we operate: assembling the right partners and sponsors around storytelling that serves the community first.

Through relationships like this one, we have genuine reach into Spanish-language radio, TV, and newspaper outlets across Colorado — not just the ability to produce bilingual content, but the network to get it placed.

Real Estate Branding & Social Content

Real Estate: Colorado Vizion, Tu Realtor, Legacy Home

Colorado Vizion — a real estate branding video produced in both English and Spanish, showcasing the brand's mission and services through authentic storytelling.

Tu Realtor (Alfredo Salcedo) — ongoing social media videos, branding videos, and video marketing support for real estate seminars and client outreach.

Legacy Home (Megastar) — professional headshots and social media video content.

Food, Beverage & Hospitality Storytelling & Events

Latin Supper Club & Mi Abuelo Organic Chile

Latin Supper Club, by the Hispanic Restaurant Association — podcast production and event coverage supporting the organization's mission to elevate Latino culinary talent across Colorado.

Mi Abuelo Organic Chile — brand storytelling video built around the company's origin and product, designed to connect with both the culture behind the brand and the customers it serves.

We've also produced branding videos for small construction businesses and training videos for schools — proof that the same strategy-first approach scales down to smaller budgets, not just large institutional projects.
